<p class="highlights-intro">Research highlights:</p>
<ul class="highlights">
  <li>
    <a href="{{ '/publications/#cachay2026ucast' | relative_url }}"><strong>U-Cast</strong></a> (ICML 2026):
    a simple U-Net-based weather forecaster that matches or exceeds GenCast and IFS ENS in probabilistic skill at 1.5&deg; resolution,
    with over 10&times; less training compute than leading CRPS-based models.
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="{{ '/publications/#cachay2024probemulation' | relative_url }}"><strong>Spherical DYffusion</strong></a>
    (NeurIPS 2024 Spotlight; Best Paper, ICML ML4ESM workshop):
    the first conditional generative model to produce accurate, physically consistent global climate ensemble simulations,
    stable over 100-year rollouts.
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="{{ '/publications/#cachay2023dyffusion' | relative_url }}"><strong>DYffusion</strong></a> (NeurIPS 2023) and
    <a href="{{ '/publications/#cachay2025erdm' | relative_url }}"><strong>ERDM</strong></a> (NeurIPS 2025):
    diffusion frameworks for probabilistic forecasting of complex spatiotemporal dynamics.
  </li>
</ul>
